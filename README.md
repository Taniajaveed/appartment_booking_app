# 🏡 — Apartment Booking App

> **Find your perfect stay, book with confidence.**

**StayNest** is a modern Flutter apartment booking application designed to make discovering and booking comfortable apartments simple, fast, and convenient. The app provides a clean, trendy interface with a smooth booking experience from property discovery to payment.

---

## ✨ Features

* 🏠 **Explore Apartments** — Browse available apartments and properties.
* 🔍 **Search & Discover** — Find properties based on your preferences.
* ❤️ **Favorites** — Save apartments you love for later.
* 📍 **Property Details** — View apartment information, images, facilities, and pricing.
* 📅 **Easy Booking** — Select your stay details and reserve your apartment.
* 💳 **Booking & Payment Flow** — Simple and user-friendly checkout experience.
* 🧾 **Booking Details** — Keep track of your reservations.
* 🌙 **Dark & Light Theme** — Comfortable UI in both themes.
* 📱 **Responsive UI** — Optimized for different mobile screen sizes.
* 🌐 **Localization Ready** — Structured to support multiple languages.
* 📶 **Offline Awareness** — Displays network connectivity status.
* 🔙 **Double Back to Exit** — Prevents accidental app closure.

## 🏗️ Architecture

The project follows a structured and scalable approach to keep the UI, business logic, and data models separated.

```text
Presentation
     │
     ├── Screens
     ├── Widgets
     └── UI Components
     │
     ↓
State Management
     │
     ├── GetX
     │
     ↓
Data Layer
     │
     ├── Models
     ├── controllers
```

This makes the project easier to **maintain, test, scale, and extend**.
